About Us

Delivering Trust Since 2011

Express Corporation of India is one of India's fastest-growing courier and logistics companies, connecting businesses and individuals across the country with fast, secure delivery.

Our Story

Incorporated in November 2011 with just 10 branches, Express Corporation of India has grown into a nationwide network of over 500 branches, serving more than 4,000 pin codes and handling around 1.5 million transactions every day.

What started as a small regional courier operation has become a trusted logistics partner for e-commerce businesses, enterprises, and individuals who need fast, dependable delivery across India and abroad.

Our Mission

To provide fast, reliable and affordable courier and logistics solutions, backed by technology that gives every customer full visibility into their shipment's journey.

We combine an extensive branch network with a modern, easy-to-use online tracking platform, so you always know where your parcel is - from pickup to final delivery.
